From your resume to an offer, one card at a time.

RoleHawk reads your resume, finds jobs that genuinely fit you, writes a CV and cover letter for each one, and keeps every application moving. Claude does the heavy lifting. You make every call, and your data stays in a folder you own.

Three things it will never do

A job search is high stakes. These are the lines RoleHawk is built never to cross.

Your CV

Invent a fact

Every employer, title, date, degree and number comes from your profile. Three checks flag anything that doesn't match, before you ever send it.

Applying

Submit for you

It drafts your documents and can fill in a form, then stops. You read it, and you press submit.

Your data

Phone home

No RoleHawk account, server, analytics or telemetry. It talks to Claude on your key and the job sources you turn on.

How it works

Your whole search, in the order you live it

Each step hands its work to the next, so you never type the same thing twice, and you always know what to do next.

01 · Profile

Start with the resume you already have

Bring a PDF, a Word file, plain text or your LinkedIn export. A short interview fills in the gaps, like the project you're proudest of or the commute you won't do. Nothing is saved until you've read it and said yes.

02 · Discover

Find the jobs worth your evening

RoleHawk searches Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable boards for a watchlist of companies you choose, plus Remote OK, We Work Remotely, Adzuna and USAJOBS. You can add any careers page yourself.

Every job is scored on the same rubric of experience, skills, goals and logistics, and every score comes with its reasons. You see the cost before it runs.

03 · Apply

A tailored CV and letter, and every word of it true

One click writes both, as Word and PDF, from templates applicant tracking systems can read. Drafts pull your roles straight from your profile. A fact check flags any number or skill it can't find there, and a second review marks stretches and generic lines, for you to fix or keep.

It also checks the posting's requirements against your CV, so you know what's missing before they do.

04 · Track

Nothing slips through the cracks

Every application lives on the board you played with above, from Saved to Offer. Each card tells you the one thing to do next: write it, check a flagged line, follow up, or get ready for Thursday.

After ten quiet days, RoleHawk drafts a follow-up from what you actually sent. You send it, from your own inbox. RoleHawk never sends anything.

Setup

Up and running in about ten minutes

  1. 1

    Install with one line

    About three minutes, no admin rights, and no Python or Node to set up. It opens in your browser when it's done.

  2. 2

    Connect Claude

    Make an API key on the Claude Platform, add $10 to $20 of credit, and set a monthly limit. The app walks you through it.

  3. 3

    Add your resume and search

    Import a resume or LinkedIn export, check your profile, pick your target roles, and let it find and rank jobs. Then write your first application.

Install with one line

bash curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/travist6983/rolehawk/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
PowerShell irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/travist6983/rolehawk/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex

Everything installs in your home folder, with no admin rights, and the installer checks the download's checksum before unpacking. Read the scripts first if you like. Want a look before you set anything up? Run the demo. It needs no API key.

What it costs

RoleHawk is free. Claude is pay as you go.

The AI runs on your own key from the Claude Platform, so you pay Anthropic only for what you use. Ranking uses Claude Haiku and costs cents. Writing applications uses Claude Opus, and that's most of the bill.

Batch jobs show an estimate before they run, Insights shows what you've spent, and a monthly cap in Settings stops new AI calls when you hit it. No surprises.

Questions you might have

Is it really free?

Yes. RoleHawk is open source under the MIT license, with no paid plan and no account. The only cost is the Claude usage you choose to run on your own API key.

Can I use my Claude Pro or Max subscription?

Not inside the app, because other apps can't sign in with a Claude subscription. If you use Claude Code, though, you can run every RoleHawk command there through its skills, and the thinking happens in your Claude Code session.

Will it apply to jobs for me?

No, and that's on purpose. It drafts your documents and can fill in an application form, then stops so you can read everything and press submit yourself.

Will it make things up on my CV?

It's built not to. Employers, titles, dates, degrees, certifications and numbers come only from your profile. Anything the checks can't match is highlighted before you use the document.

Does it work without an API key?

Partly. Importing a LinkedIn export, the setup interview, job search, the pipeline, insights and reports all work. Reading a resume, ranking, writing and chat need Claude.

Does it search LinkedIn or Indeed?

Not yet. Searching them breaks their terms, so they'll come later as opt-in sources, clearly marked experimental. Many of the same jobs are on the companies' own boards, which RoleHawk does search.

Where is my data, and how do I move it?

In a RoleHawk folder in your home folder: your profile and memory as markdown files, your documents, backups, and one SQLite database. Copy the folder to move it.

Which countries does it support?

Any. Job sources are filtered by the countries you search in, and Adzuna covers 19 countries once you add its free key.

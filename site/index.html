Your job search,
on your computer.

RoleHawk is an AI job search copilot. It reads your resume, finds and ranks jobs from company career pages and job boards, writes a tailored CV and cover letter for each one without inventing anything, and tracks every application. Claude does the thinking. Your data stays in a folder you own.

Every screen on this page shows made-up demo data: a fictional job seeker and fictional companies.

Never invents a fact

Employers, titles, dates, degrees, and numbers come only from your profile. Three checks flag anything that doesn't match before you use it.

Never submits for you

It drafts documents and can fill in an application form, then stops. You read it and press submit yourself.

Never phones home

No RoleHawk account, server, analytics, or telemetry. It talks only to Claude on your key and the job sources you turn on.

What it does

From a resume to an offer, in one place

Your whole search on one board

Every application moves from Saved to Offer, by drag or keyboard. Each card shows its fit score and the one thing to do next: write the application, check a flagged line, send a follow-up after ten quiet days, or prepare for an interview.

Find and rank jobs that fit

RoleHawk searches Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and Workable boards for a watchlist of companies you control, plus Remote OK, We Work Remotely, Adzuna, and USAJOBS. Add any careers page yourself. One fit rubric scores experience, skills, goals, and logistics, explains every score, and shows the cost before it runs.

A tailored CV and cover letter, fact checked

One click writes both, as Word and PDF, from templates that applicant tracking systems can read. Drafts pick your roles by reference, a fact check flags unknown numbers and skills, and an AI review marks stretches and generic lines for you to fix or keep. It checks the posting's requirements against your CV too.

See what's working

Insights shows your funnel, response rate by source, weekly activity, and what you've spent on Claude this month. Export a self-contained report to share with a mentor or career coach.

Ask Claude from any screen

Chat knows your profile, your pipeline, and the page you're on. It remembers what you tell it, like preferences, deal breakers, and stories, in plain markdown files, and never changes them without your OK.

  • Builds your profile from a resume (PDF, Word, or text) or a LinkedIn data export, then fills the gaps with a short interview. You review everything first.
  • Follow-ups drafted from what you actually sent, for you to send yourself.
  • Learns from results. As replies and rejections come in, it suggests changes to your preferences for you to accept or reject.
  • Light or dark, following your system. Backups and updates never touch your data without a copy first.

Local first, on purpose

Your resume, your pipeline, and your key stay with you

A job search is personal. RoleHawk runs on your own computer and keeps everything in a folder you can open, read, back up, or delete.

  • Runs on 127.0.0.1. A random token in the launch link keeps other web pages out of it.
  • Talks to a short list. Anthropic with your key, the job sources you turn on, employer pages you open with autofill, and GitHub when you update. Settings lists every one.
  • Your key goes in the keychain of your operating system, never a file.
  • Plain files. Your profile and memory are markdown, plus one SQLite database. Copy the folder to move it.
  • Postings can't boss Claude around. Hidden text is stripped, and lines aimed at AI tools are shown to you, never obeyed.
  • Open source. Read every line on GitHub under the MIT license.

How it works

Running in about ten minutes

  1. 1

    Install with one line

    About three minutes, no admin rights, and no Python or Node to set up yourself. It opens in your browser when it's done.

  2. 2

    Connect Claude

    Make an API key on the Claude Platform, add $10 to $20 of credit, paste it in, and set a monthly limit. The app walks you through it. Use Claude Code? Skip the key and use the built-in skills.

  3. 3

    Add your resume and search

    Import a resume or LinkedIn export, review your profile, pick your target roles, and let it find and rank jobs. Then write your first application.

Install

One line in a terminal

macOS or Linux, in Terminal

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/travist6983/rolehawk/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

Windows, in PowerShell

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/travist6983/rolehawk/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex

Everything installs in your home folder, and the installer checks the download's checksum before unpacking it. Read the scripts first if you like. Want to look around before setting it up? Run the demo, which needs no API key.

What it costs

RoleHawk is free. Claude is pay as you go.

The AI runs on your own API key from the Claude Platform, so you pay Anthropic only for what you use. Ranking uses Claude Haiku and costs cents. Writing applications uses Claude Opus and is most of the bill.

Batch commands show an estimate before they run, Insights shows what you've spent, and a monthly cap in Settings stops new AI calls when you reach it.

Questions

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. RoleHawk is open source under the MIT license, with no paid plan and no account. The only cost is the Claude usage you choose to run on your own API key, shown above.

Can I use my Claude Pro or Max subscription?

Not inside the app, because other apps can't sign in with a Claude subscription. If you use Claude Code, though, you can run every RoleHawk command there through its skills, and the reasoning happens in your Claude Code session.

Does it apply to jobs automatically?

No. It drafts documents and fills in forms, then stops so you can check everything and press submit yourself.

Will it make things up on my CV?

It's built not to. Employers, titles, dates, degrees, certifications, and numbers come only from your profile. Anything the checks can't match is highlighted for you before you use the document.

Does it work without an API key?

Partly. Importing a LinkedIn export, the setup interview, job search, the pipeline, insights, and reports all work. Reading a resume, ranking, writing, and chat need Claude.

Does it search LinkedIn or Indeed?

Not yet. Searching them breaks their terms, so they'll come later as opt-in, clearly marked experimental sources. Many of the same jobs are on the companies' own boards, which RoleHawk does search.

Where is my data, and how do I move it?

In a RoleHawk folder in your home folder: your profile and memory as markdown files, your documents, backups, and one SQLite database. Copy the folder to move it.

Which countries does it support?

Any. Job sources are filtered by the countries you search in, and Adzuna covers 19 countries when you add its free key.

Who makes it?

RoleHawk is built by Travis Terry, a software engineer. It's open source, so issues, ideas, and pull requests are welcome on GitHub.

**Check everything before you send it.** RoleHawk drafts CVs, cover letters, and answers
from your own profile and checks them against it, but you are responsible for what you
submit to an employer. Read every document before you use it. Autofill fills in forms and
stops; it never submits an application for you.

**Your data stays on your computer**, in the data folder (`~/RoleHawk` by default). The
app sends data only to the places you turn on: Anthropic (to run Claude with your own API
key), the job sources you enable, and integrations you connect. Settings lists every
destination. Anthropic's handling of what you send is covered by its own terms and privacy
policy.
